How It Works

TruthGuard uses advanced Natural Language Processing to detect patterns common in misinformation.

  1. 1. Input Text: You provide the article text or headline.
  2. 2. AI Processing: We tokenize the words and evaluate their usage frequencies (TF-IDF).
  3. 3. Verdict: A mathematical margin evaluates it against our trained dataset matrix.
  4. 4. Insights: You are provided a final output of either Authentic or Fake.
