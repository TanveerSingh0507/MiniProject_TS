Introduction:-
The advent of social media and digital news platforms has fundamentally transformed
how information is consumed and disseminated globally. While this democratization of
information has empowered billions, it has also created fertile ground for the spread of
misinformation. Fake news — fabricated or misleading content presented as factual
journalism — poses a significant threat to democracy, public health, and social cohesion.
Traditional fact-checking methods are manual, slow, and unable to scale to the billions
of articles published online every day. Automated fake news detection using Natural
Language Processing (NLP) and machine learning has therefore emerged as a critical area of
research and application. By training models on large, labeled datasets of verified and
unverified news, it is possible to build systems that can classify news articles with high
accuracy within milliseconds.
TruthGuard is a response to this challenge. It is a full-stack, AI-powered web
application that allows any user to paste a news article or headline and receive an instant
verdict — "Real" or "Fake" — backed by a machine learning model trained on thousands of
fact-checked examples. Beyond classification, TruthGuard provides a secure, authenticated
environment where users can track their analysis history and revisit previous checks.
